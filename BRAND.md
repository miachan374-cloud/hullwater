# Hullwater brand decisions

## Colors

| Token | Hex | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White | #FFFFFF | Input fields and the button text |
| Cream | #FBFAF7 | Page background: a near-white that's barely warm, not a yellow cream |
| Blue | #CBCFD9 | The tile behind the can-top photo |
| Light blue | #E3E4E8 | Halfway between blue and cream. The background of the Lucy Liu story section (dark olive text, 6.9:1) |
| Pink | #D5B9B2 | Not used at the moment |
| Olive | #78705E | All text and the main button (4.9:1 on white) |
| Baby blue | #C9E4F6 | The accent: behind the ingredients and the scrolling banner (replaced the green #D0F0C0) |
| Dark olive | #4F4A3E | Text on the banner and the blue story section, where regular olive is too faint |

Blue, pink and baby blue are too light to use as text colors. Olive text on blue is 3.1:1, which is too low for body text.

## Type

- Shippori Mincho for the wordmark, headings and ingredients.
- EB Garamond for paragraphs, the waitlist form and the footer.
- Both replaced Times New Roman, and are sized so every line is the same length it was in Times.
- The scrolling banner uses Space Mono, from Google Fonts.
- Normal capitalization only: sentence case, with no all-caps labels and no small caps.

## The one bold element

- Three photos in a row, with no borders and no background panel:
  - Center and largest: `images/can-opening.jpg`, a hand opening a cold can. The can in this photo isn't a Hullwater can, so replace it once there's a real one to photograph.
  - Left: `images/cans-on-ice.jpg`, plain silver cans on ice.
  - Right: `images/can-top.jpg`, the top of a can on a solid #CBCFD9 blue tile. It sits behind the center photo.

## What the page says

- The product: cold-brewed barley tea.
- The ingredients: water, barley and hojicha. Matcha was removed. The founder's brief says "four ingredients," so confirm the count with them.
- What the visitor does: joins the waitlist.

## Sections

- Cut: the dashboard, the logo row and the stats.
- Cut: the features, steps, testimonials, FAQ, "Did you know?" callout and final call to action. The founder gave nothing to support them.
- Kept: the first screen (wordmark, photo collage, ingredients and waitlist), the story of the founder, Lucy Liu, a sourcing section, and a one-row footer.
- Cut later: the waitlist price section.

## Facts from the founder

- Founder: Lucy Liu (she/her).
- Hojicha: the leaves are picked by hand in Uji, Japan, in the spring.
- Hojicha roasting: roasted in Japan by roasters with many years of experience.
- Barley: from Antler Valley Farm in Alberta, Canada, and roasted before brewing. Roasting gives a stronger aroma and draws out antioxidants (needs a source).
- Brewing: steeped in cold water for 48 hours to bring out the most flavor.
- Sourcing: every ingredient comes from local growers.
- Certified organic by the USDA.
- Price: $16 for a 6-pack, shown as one line under the waitlist button.
