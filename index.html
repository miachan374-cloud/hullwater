Grandma’s recipe

Lucy Liu would get back from a run and find nothing in the fridge aisle she wanted to drink. Everything was either sugar or aggressively healthy. Every summer, her grandmother made barley and hojicha tea. Just barley, hojicha, and nothing else.

Lucy craved it all year round, and now you will too.

Our sourcing

We buy every ingredient from local growers in Uji, Japan, and Alberta, Canada. Hullwater is certified organic by the USDA.

Barley

Our barley comes from Antler Valley Farm in Alberta. The barley is roasted before brewing, which brings out a stronger, toasted aroma. Roasting also draws antioxidants out of the grain.

Hojicha

Tea farmers in Uji pick the leaves for our hojicha by hand in the spring, while the new leaves are still soft and green. Experienced Japanese roasters then time each roast to keep the leaves as fresh as possible.

Brewing

We steep the roasted hojicha and barley in cold water for 48 hours, which is long enough to bring out the full flavor of both.
